How FINPULSE AI works

FINPULSE AI turns a month of spending, or one period of accounts, into a diagnosis: what the position is, why, and what to do first. It has three layers.

  • Rules and formulas. Every ratio, date and budget line is a fixed calculation you can trace. The business diagnosis is entirely rule-based.
  • A trained model. The student early warning is a logistic regression with six inputs. It estimates the chance that the balance falls under 5% of the allowance five or more days before month end.
  • A language model, optional. The server version can rewrite a result in friendlier words. This website does not use it. The explanations here are built from the calculated figures.

Limits

  • All training and sample data is synthetic. Accuracy on real students is not known yet.
  • Business thresholds are general rules of thumb and differ by industry.
  • Any finding that needs urgent action caps the business score at 44, so a strong average cannot hide a cash shortage.
  • A measure with missing inputs is left out and listed. Nothing is guessed.
  • FINPULSE AI gives education and scenarios. It never names a share, fund or scheme, and it never promises a return.
